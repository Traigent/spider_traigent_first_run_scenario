SQLite databases that ship with this project are not original work. They come from the
Spider 1.0 text-to-SQL dataset and are redistributed here in modified form, under the

Notice of modification
----------------------

Required by section 3(a)(1)(B) of the licence, and worth reading on its own account:

    THIS IS NOT THE SPIDER DATASET. It is a modified subset of it.

What that means concretely:

  * Rows were selected, not taken whole. Only some of the original records are here,
    drawn from one split of the original release, over some of that split's databases,
    and only the databases those records refer to. Whole databases, and every question
    asked about them, are absent.

  * Records were reshaped. They are carried in a different record format from the one
    the original release uses, and they carry fields the original does not -- among them
    identifiers, groupings and labels applied here rather than upstream.

  * Records were edited. Any individual record in this copy may differ from its
    counterpart in the original release, and no record here should be assumed to
    reproduce one.

  * The databases are the original SQLite files as distributed with the dataset, carried
    only for the records that need them.

None of this is a defect in the original. It is what was done to it to arrive at this
copy, and it is stated so that nobody mistakes one for the other.


Using this data honestly
------------------------

A score measured here is a score on this modified subset. It is not a score on Spider,
it is not comparable with published Spider results, and it should not be reported as
one. Where a number matters, measure it against the original release at the project URI
above.
